# patterns.packet

**Page job:** Turn Nuwa's moves into reusable skill-writing patterns.

**Reader state:** The reader is ready to steal moves for another skill.

**Voice:** Pattern cards, but with ordinary names.

**Evidence shape:** Problem, therefore break, use when, reuse details, anti-example, cost, seen-in, related patterns.

**Failure mode:** Renamed section headings pretending to be patterns.

**Pain scan rows used:** All major rows, especially source quality, checkpointing, protocol, validation.

**Inputs:** `references/cards-patterns.md`, `handbook-brief.md`, source analysis.

**Must include:** Therefore break and at least one related pattern per card.

**Must avoid:** A flat list with no costs.

**Packet output:** `handbook.patterns[]` and `pattern-network.svg`.

**Self-check:** Reader can steal at least three moves immediately.
