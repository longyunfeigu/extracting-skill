# design-choices.packet

**Page job:** Explain why major Nuwa rules exist and which bad output they block.

**Reader state:** The reader wants to understand not just copy the rules.

**Voice:** Three-act structure: AI default, skill constraint, result.

**Evidence shape:** Design choice cards with bad scenario, constraint, solved problem, reusable move, three counter-scenarios.

**Failure mode:** Generic best practices or a list of source headings.

**Pain scan rows used:** Route before work, checkpoints, triple validation, Agentic Protocol, scripts.

**Inputs:** `SKILL.md`, `references/cards-patterns.md`, brief design choices.

**Must include:** At least one case where each choice is too heavy or unnecessary.

**Must avoid:** All-green scenarios.

**Packet output:** `handbook.designChoices[]`.

**Self-check:** Every card names the specific bad result prevented.
